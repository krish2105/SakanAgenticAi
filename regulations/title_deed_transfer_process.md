## TITLE-1: Scope — Ready Properties vs. Completed Off-Plan

This process governs the transfer of a title deed for a ready (completed, previously-titled) property between a seller and buyer, and the initial title deed issuance for a completed off-plan unit converting from Oqood pre-registration. Both paths converge on the same registration record format, but ready-property transfers additionally require settlement of any outstanding mortgage or service-charge lien against the current title before transfer can register.

## TITLE-2: No-Objection Certificate (NOC)

Before a ready-property transfer may proceed, the seller must obtain a No-Objection Certificate from the building's managing developer or owners' association, confirming there are no outstanding service charges, utility arrears, or building-level disputes attached to the unit. The NOC is valid for 30 days from issuance; a transfer attempted after expiry requires a fresh NOC.

## TITLE-3: Transfer Execution and Registration

Transfer requires both parties (or authorized representatives) to appear before the registration authority, present the signed sale agreement, current title deed, valid identification, and the NOC. Where the property is mortgaged, the existing lender's clearance letter or a simultaneous mortgage-settlement transaction must be presented. Upon execution, the registration authority issues a new title deed in the buyer's name and cancels the seller's prior deed record.

## TITLE-4: Transfer Fees

Standard transfer fees are calculated as a percentage of the higher of the declared sale price or the registration authority's assessed property value, split between buyer and seller per the sale agreement (commonly borne by the buyer unless otherwise negotiated). Off-plan units converting from Oqood at project completion are charged the equivalent registration fee at the time of initial Oqood filing, not again at final title issuance, provided no ownership change has occurred in the interim.

## TITLE-5: Foreign Buyer Considerations

Transfers to foreign (non-GCC national) buyers are permitted only within designated freehold areas; see Foreign Ownership in Freehold Areas for the applicable community list and any additional documentation the registration authority requires for non-resident buyers, including source-of-funds declarations above specified value thresholds.
