## AIDISC-1: Scope of the Disclosure Requirement

Effective 2026, any property listing, brochure, or digital advertisement that includes AI-generated property descriptions, computer-generated renderings presented as photography, or AI-modified imagery of a unit, building, or amenity must carry a clear, visible disclosure identifying the AI-generated content as such. This applies equally to ready and off-plan listings, and to content produced by the developer, the listing broker, or a third-party marketing agency acting on their behalf.

## AIDISC-2: Form of Disclosure

The disclosure must appear adjacent to the affected content (not buried in a general terms-and-conditions footer), using plain-language wording such as "AI-generated rendering — not an exact representation" or "Property description generated with AI assistance; verify unit specifications with the listing agent." A disclosure that is present but not reasonably visible to a buyer viewing the listing on a standard mobile device does not satisfy this requirement.

## AIDISC-3: Distinguishing Disclosure from Accuracy Obligations

This disclosure obligation is independent of, and does not substitute for, the marketing content accuracy requirements in OFFPLAN-4 or the standard consumer-protection prohibition on materially misleading property descriptions. A listing may be properly disclosed as AI-generated and still be non-compliant if the underlying content misrepresents the unit's actual specifications, floor area, or view.

## AIDISC-4: Enforcement and Remedies

A listing found non-compliant with the disclosure requirement may be ordered removed from public marketing channels pending correction. Where a buyer can demonstrate reliance on an undisclosed AI-generated representation that materially misrepresented the property, the buyer's remedies run through the same channels available for conventional misleading-marketing claims (e.g., agreement rescission grounds under Form F, where applicable), rather than through a separate AI-specific penalty regime.

## AIDISC-5: Recordkeeping

Developers and brokerages must retain records identifying which listing assets were AI-generated or AI-modified, and the disclosure applied to each, for a minimum of 24 months from the listing's removal or the underlying transaction's completion, whichever is later, to support any subsequent compliance review.
