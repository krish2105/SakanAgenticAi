## ESCROW-1: Mandatory Escrow for Off-Plan Sales

No developer may collect any payment from a buyer — reservation deposit, instalment, or otherwise — for an off-plan unit outside of a project-specific escrow account held with a regulator-approved trustee bank. Commingling of funds across multiple projects within a single escrow account is prohibited even where the projects share a common developer entity; each Form A-registered project requires its own segregated account.

## ESCROW-2: Trustee Bank Obligations

The trustee bank must independently verify each disbursement request against the certified construction-completion percentage supplied by the project's appointed engineering consultant before releasing funds to the developer. The trustee bank is jointly liable, alongside the developer, for any disbursement made without such verification, and must submit quarterly account statements to the regulator showing inflows, outflows, and the account's reconciliation against reported construction progress.

## ESCROW-3: Retention Reserve

Five percent of total collected funds must remain in the escrow account until the regulator has confirmed unit registration (title deed issuance) is complete for all units in the project, providing a buyer-protection reserve against post-handover completion defects or unresolved service-charge disputes at the community level.

## ESCROW-4: Escrow Status Disclosure in Marketing and Resale

A project's current escrow account status (Active, Under Review, or Suspended) must be disclosed to any prospective buyer or assignee prior to reservation or resale of an off-plan interest, and is publicly queryable against the project's registration number. A status of "Under Review" or "Suspended" does not itself void existing agreements but must be flagged in any compliance summary produced for a deal involving that project, since it materially affects the developer's ability to draw further construction funds.

## ESCROW-5: Escrow Closure and Fund Release to Developer

Upon full completion, unit handover, and title deed issuance for all units in a project, the trustee bank closes the escrow account and releases the retention reserve to the developer, net of any amounts held pending unresolved buyer disputes that were formally lodged prior to closure. Closure must be reported to the regulator within 30 days.
