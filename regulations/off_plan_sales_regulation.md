## OFFPLAN-1: Eligibility to Market and Sell Off-Plan

A developer may begin marketing an off-plan project only after Form A registration is approved and the project's escrow account is opened and confirmed active. Any advertisement, brochure, or digital listing published before these two conditions are met is a regulatory violation regardless of whether a sale was actually concluded, and any resulting reservation is void.

## OFFPLAN-2: Construction-Linked Payment Plans

Off-plan payment plans must link at least 60% of the total contract value to physical construction milestones certified by an independent engineering consultant, rather than to calendar dates alone. Purely time-based "post-handover" plans exceeding 40% of contract value require additional developer collateral to be posted with the regulator, disclosed to the buyer as part of the Form F schedule.

## OFFPLAN-3: Secondary Market Resale of Off-Plan Units

Resale (assignment) of an off-plan unit prior to handover is permitted only where: the original buyer has paid at least 30% of the contract value into escrow; the resale is registered with the regulator as an assignment against the existing Form F; and the incoming buyer receives the same disclosures (escrow status, construction percentage, delay-remedy terms) that applied to the original reservation. Unregistered "off-market" assignments carry no title protection.

## OFFPLAN-4: Marketing Content Accuracy

Renderings, floor plans, and unit specifications used in marketing must match the registered Form A project specification. Where actual delivered specifications differ from marketing materials beyond the variance tolerance in the Form F sale agreement, the developer bears disclosure and remedy obligations under RERA-F-5, independent of any marketing-content violation penalty.

## OFFPLAN-5: Interaction with AI-Generated Marketing Content

Off-plan marketing materials that include AI-generated renderings, descriptions, or imagery are additionally subject to the disclosure obligations in the AI-Generated Marketing Disclosure regulation (AIDISC), which apply cumulatively with, not in place of, the accuracy requirements in OFFPLAN-4.
