## MORTGAGE-1: Registration as a Condition of Enforceability

A mortgage over a property — ready or off-plan (against an Oqood-registered interest) — is only enforceable against third parties once it is registered against the title record or Oqood pre-registration entry. An unregistered mortgage is a private contractual arrangement between borrower and lender with no priority claim against the property itself, and cannot block a subsequent unencumbered sale to a good-faith buyer.

## MORTGAGE-2: Loan-to-Value Limits

Standard loan-to-value caps apply based on buyer residency status and whether the property is the buyer's first purchase: UAE resident first-time buyers are typically capped higher than non-resident or subsequent-purchase buyers, and off-plan mortgages carry additional lender-specific caps tied to the project's construction-completion percentage, since most lenders will not fully fund a mortgage against a project below a minimum completion threshold.

## MORTGAGE-3: Off-Plan Mortgage Interaction with Escrow

Where a buyer finances an off-plan purchase, the lender's funds are disbursed into the same project escrow account as the buyer's own instalments, following the same construction-milestone verification process described in the Escrow Account Regulations. The lender's interest is noted on the Oqood pre-registration per OQOOD-2, and any subsequent resale or refinancing must clear the existing lender's no-objection letter before a new mortgage or full transfer may register.

## MORTGAGE-4: Mortgage Settlement on Sale

A mortgaged property may not complete a title transfer under the Title Deed Transfer Process until the outstanding mortgage balance is settled, either by the seller from sale proceeds at the point of transfer or by the buyer's new lender directly to the existing lender in a simultaneous settlement transaction. The registration authority requires the existing lender's clearance letter (or an escrow-held settlement undertaking) before it will cancel the existing mortgage entry and register a new one.

## MORTGAGE-5: Default and Foreclosure

Upon sustained borrower default, the lender may petition the registration authority and relevant court to enforce the mortgage, which can result in a forced sale of the property. Any compliance summary for a deal involving a distressed or bank-listed property should flag foreclosure status explicitly, since a foreclosure-stage title carries materially different transfer procedures and timelines than a standard voluntary sale.
