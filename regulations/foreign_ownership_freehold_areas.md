## FOREIGN-1: Freehold vs. Leasehold Distinction

Non-GCC national buyers may hold full freehold title (outright, transferable, inheritable ownership including the underlying land interest for apartments held on a strata basis) only within communities the regulator has specifically designated as freehold areas. Outside these designated areas, non-GCC nationals may only hold usufruct or long-term leasehold rights, typically capped at 99 years, which are a materially different and lesser interest than freehold title.

## FOREIGN-2: Designated Freehold Communities

Designated freehold areas include, among others, Dubai Marina, Downtown Dubai, Business Bay, Jumeirah Village Circle, Palm Jumeirah, Dubai Hills Estate, Jumeirah Lake Towers, Arabian Ranches, DAMAC Hills, Al Furjan, Motor City, Dubai Silicon Oasis, Dubai South, and Mohammed Bin Rashid City. A compliance check for any deal must confirm the subject community appears on the current designated list before representing a foreign buyer's interest as freehold-eligible; the list is periodically expanded and a community's absence from a given corpus snapshot should not be treated as a definitive present-day exclusion without a live registry check.

## FOREIGN-3: Documentation for Non-Resident Buyers

Non-resident foreign buyers must provide a valid passport, and for purchases above the regulator's specified value threshold, a source-of-funds declaration and, where payment originates from outside the country, evidence of the international funds transfer. Corporate foreign buyers must additionally provide incorporation documents and a board resolution authorizing the purchase.

## FOREIGN-4: GCC National Buyers

GCC nationals are not subject to the freehold-area restriction and may hold freehold title anywhere non-GCC nationals may also purchase, plus certain additional zones reserved for GCC and UAE nationals only. A compliance summary should not assume GCC-national eligibility is identical to non-GCC foreign-buyer eligibility without confirming the buyer's nationality classification.

## FOREIGN-5: Effect on Financing

Mortgage financing for non-resident foreign buyers is available through regulator-licensed lenders but is typically capped at a lower loan-to-value ratio than for UAE resident buyers, and lender due diligence for source-of-funds compliance runs in parallel with, not in substitution for, the title registration authority's own source-of-funds review under FOREIGN-3.
