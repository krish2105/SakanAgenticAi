## RERA-F-1: Nature and Precedence of Form F

Form F is the standard, regulator-issued sale and purchase agreement template for off-plan units. Developers may append project-specific schedules (payment plan, unit specification, service charge estimate) but may not alter the regulator's core clauses governing buyer protections, escrow mechanics, or delay remedies. Where a developer's bespoke agreement conflicts with the Form F core clauses, the Form F terms prevail for any unit registered against a project with an active Form A.

## RERA-F-2: Registration Requirement for Enforceability

A Form F agreement is not enforceable against the project's escrow account or eligible for Oqood pre-registration until it has been lodged with the regulator and issued a sale agreement registration number. Unregistered agreements do not entitle the buyer to file a claim through the Rental Dispute Center's off-plan equivalent process, and any resale of an unregistered interest is prohibited.

## RERA-F-3: Payment Plan and Milestone Linkage

Each payment instalment under Form F must be tied to either a calendar milestone or a verified construction-completion percentage, never to the developer's discretion alone. The escrow trustee bank releases funds to the developer only upon independent engineering-consultant certification that the corresponding construction milestone has been reached, capped at the percentage specified in the Escrow Account Regulations.

## RERA-F-4: Handover Delay Remedies

Where handover is delayed beyond the grace period stated in the agreement (typically 6–12 months from the contracted handover date, project-dependent), the buyer may elect to: (a) continue the agreement and receive a compensation credit calculated per the delay-remedy schedule; or (b) terminate and receive a full refund of amounts paid into escrow, plus accrued compensation, within 60 days of the termination notice. A developer may not condition either remedy on the buyer waiving other statutory rights.

## RERA-F-5: Unit Specification Variance

The final delivered unit's floor area may vary from the Form F specification by no more than 5% without triggering a mandatory price adjustment in the buyer's favor (or, where the unit is larger, the developer's favor subject to buyer consent). Variance beyond 5% entitles the buyer to a proportional price adjustment or, at variance beyond 10%, contract rescission with full refund.
