## RERA-B-1: When Form B Is Required

Form B documents a buyer's reservation of a specific unit within a project that already holds an approved Form A registration. It is a preliminary instrument, not a transfer of title, and precedes the binding Form F sale and purchase agreement. A developer may not accept a reservation deposit from a buyer, nor represent a unit as "sold" or "reserved" in any marketing material, without a corresponding Form B on file referencing the project's RERA registration number.

## RERA-B-2: Reservation Deposit Handling

Any deposit collected under a Form B reservation must be paid directly into the project's designated escrow account, not to the developer's general operating account or to any broker intermediary. The maximum permissible reservation deposit is 5% of the unit's listed sale price unless the full Form F agreement is executed within the same transaction, in which case the standard off-plan payment plan schedule governs. Deposits held under a Form B that does not convert to a Form F within 60 days are refundable to the buyer within 14 business days of a written cancellation request, less any documented marketing costs disclosed at reservation.

## RERA-B-3: Mandatory Disclosures at Reservation

At the point of reservation, the developer must disclose to the buyer, in writing: the unit's exact floor area (procedure area) and any variance tolerance permitted at handover; the project's current construction completion percentage as last certified by the appointed engineering consultant; the anticipated handover date and any penalty regime for developer delay; and the identity of the escrow trustee bank. Failure to make these disclosures renders the reservation voidable at the buyer's election within 30 days of discovering the omission.

## RERA-B-4: Conversion to Form F

A Form B reservation converts to a binding Form F sale and purchase agreement once the buyer has met the initial payment milestone specified in the project's registered payment plan. The converted Form F must reference the original Form B reservation number for audit continuity, and any unit-level price change between reservation and conversion must be separately disclosed and countersigned by the buyer.
